TendCam Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

TendCam turns an iPhone or iPad into a camera on your home network and lets another device watch it. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: TendCam collects nothing. Your video and sound never leave your own network.

What we collect

Nothing. TendCam has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive any information from the app, and there are no third-party SDKs in it.

How your video and sound travel

  • When you use a phone as a camera, it sends video and (optionally) sound directly to the devices on your local network that you allow to watch. There is no server in between and nothing is uploaded to the internet.
  • When you use TendCam to watch a camera, the video comes straight from that camera over your local network.
  • The picture and sound are not encrypted on the local network, so use TendCam on a network you trust, such as your own home Wi-Fi, and not on public or shared networks.
  • Anyone who wants to watch needs the username and password you set in the app. Without a password, anyone on your network could watch, and the app warns you about this.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to capture the video it streams from your phone.
  • Microphone: to include sound in the stream, to let a viewer talk through the phone's speaker, and to listen for crying or loud noise when you turn on alerts.
  • Local network: to find cameras on your network and to serve the stream to viewers on it.
  • Notifications: to tell you when an alert happens.
  • Background audio: so the viewing phone can keep listening for alerts, and the camera phone can keep streaming, while the screen is off. You can stop this at any time from the Alerts tab.

Alerts

Crying and noise alerts are worked out on the viewing phone itself, using the sound recognition built into iOS. The sound is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The app keeps a short history of alerts (the time, the camera name and the kind of alert) on your phone only.

What is stored on your phone

Your settings, the camera name, the alert history and the stream password are stored on your device. The password is kept in the iOS Keychain. This information stays on your device and is removed if you delete the app. If you use device backups, iOS may include the app's settings in them under your own backup settings.

Children

TendCam does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. It has no accounts and sends nothing to the developer.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated before the change is released, and the date above will change.
